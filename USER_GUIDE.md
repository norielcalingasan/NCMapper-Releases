# Contributor guide

## Start with a preset

Open **My contributions → New building → Preset & size**. Select a roof/building preset and enter width, depth, floor count and estimated floor height. The width direction is clockwise from north. Enter the geographic centre explicitly and apply the preset.

Windows and a front door are suggested automatically unless the option is turned off. These are unconfirmed suggestions. **Apply size, bearing & centre** resizes an existing design while preserving its floor/opening identities. Recheck positions after movement or resizing; the application invalidates moved-opening confirmation and reported boundary accuracy.

Compare actual occupied walls with the ground footprint. Roof overhangs and the land parcel are separate. Adjust irregular outlines and upper-floor setbacks with the outline/floor tools. A roof deck does not add an occupied floor. Leave unknown dimensions unknown.

## Position and openings

**Position & accuracy** records the source, checked date, optional supported boundary accuracy and private reference. A captured device fix locates the device; it neither locates the building centre automatically nor establishes boundary accuracy. A separate explicit action can use that point as the draft centre. Device fixes and references stay private.

**Doors & windows** places each opening on a floor, wall and measured centre offset from that wall's first corner. Use the wall/corner labels to avoid reversing the offset direction. Enter available width/height and window sill height. Confirm an opening only after checking the site or reliable plan. Record reported fire exits explicitly; no exit safety or compliance is certified.

## Save and share

Complete building name/neutral label, observation date and source. Valid edits save to your private server draft; check the save status before leaving. Failed or conflicting saves retain an error/recovery action. Unsaved/offline edits are not guaranteed to survive a forced close.

Reopen the saved draft and check dimensions, floors and openings. Sharing starts off; windows have their own sharing choice. Confirm the actual preset footprint and floor count before submitting those fields. Choose only the fields you are allowed to share, submit and obtain another authorized reviewer's approval. Suggestions and private positioning evidence are excluded from public projection.

Ctrl+0 restores normal app size. The View menu also offers enlarged zoom and full screen. 2D remains available when optional 3D is unavailable.
